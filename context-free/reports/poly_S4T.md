# poly_S4T — three primes (n = 30): cube certificate and laminar witness families

Scripts: `scripts/poly/s4t_cube.py` (cube rays / laminar-LP), `scripts/poly/s4t_chords.py`
(naive witness chords + rectangle+chord mixed family), `scripts/poly/s4t_sparse.py` (sparse exhaustive on W_30).
Setting: E = primitive vectors, W_30 = V_2+V_3+V_5 (off W_30 handled by T1-P1, ambiguity ≤ φ(30) = 8).
A laminar piece = c + ℕ-span of non-negative vectors with |supp| ≤ 2, supports pairwise non-crossing in the
linear order 0..29 (PROOF.md §3(f)). Corollary used throughout (PROVED, elementary): if a closed cone K is a finite
union of such cones, every extreme ray of K has support ≤ 2 (an extreme ray of K is extreme in the piece containing it).

## 1. Cube certificate (PROVED negative for the character version)
Cube Q_i = {i+15α+10β+6γ}. The annihilator of V_p on the cube is span{f_χ : χ_p = 1},
f_χ = Σ_v (−1)^{χ·v} x_v; f_111 vanishes on all of W_30 (useless). Axis choice g_p = f_{e_p}: the certificate
g_2,g_3,g_5 ≠ 0 covers E∩W_30 (2000 samples, 0 uncovered, 2..30 cubes per point, COMPUTER-CHECKED).
But every sign orthant {s_p g_p ≥ 0} has extreme rays e_{100}+e_{010}+e_{001}+e_{111} and e_{000}+e_{110}+e_{101}+e_{011}
(odd/even tetrahedra lie in ker g, not sums of antipodal pairs) → support 4 → NOT laminar-realizable (all 8 orthants;
LP check: ~50% of interior samples outside every maximal laminar sub-cone). Unlike the rectangle, the cube's
"diagonal" kernel is not generated by chords. Other χ-triples: not finished (budget).

## 2. Witness-chord families (COMPUTER-CHECKED; negative)
(i) three vertex-disjoint, pairwise non-crossing witness chords {s,t}, t−s ≡ 0 mod 30/p, x_s ≠ x_t
(each region {x_hi > x_lo} has periods e_hi, e_hi+e_lo: honest laminar product).
Covers all 1500 random W_30 samples and all 61020 primitive x ∈ V_3+V_5 with 0/1 generators, but FAILS on
x = W30(a,b,c) found by local search: (4,2,3,3,5,3,2,3,3,5,2,2,3,3,5,5,2,3,3,5,2,2,3,3,5,3,2,3,3,5).
(For n = 6 the family is structurally empty: no disjoint non-crossing (3-chord, 2-chord) pair exists — this is why
the rectangle was needed.) Shared-endpoint stars ("peak"/"valley") are NOT laminar: {y_b < y_a, y_b < y_c} has
extreme ray e_a+e_b+e_c.
(ii) mixed: (i) ∪ {rectangle for a prime pair with nested diagonals, |u| ≠ |w|} × {third-prime chord in a gap of R,
outside its hull, or enclosing it}. Random W_30: ply (#certificates) 312..1555, no uncovered point; local search min 14.
Sparse exhaustive (0/1 generators, supports (2,1,1),(1,2,1),(1,1,2)): 19080 primitive, **120 uncovered**, e.g.
x = 2e_0 + e_10 + e_15 + e_20 (= a+b, a = 1_{0,15}, b = 1_{0,10,20}).

## 3. Status
(a) No laminar-realizable certificate family for E∩W_30 found; cube (χ version) provably not laminar.
(b) No cover ⇒ no ply bound.
(c) CANDIDATE (not a proof): the obstruction concentrates on vectors like 2e_0+e_10+e_15+e_20 where every
witness configuration for {2,3,5} shares or crosses at a "peak" vertex. Exact open step: show every laminar
piece ⊆ E misses some point of the cone around such x (Lemma X-type: extreme rays of support ≥ 3 forced).
Caution: literature (devil §4, LITERATURE-UNVERIFIED) asserts Q∩(ab*)^30 is CF; if so, a non-certificate
(larger-support / non-sign-cone) construction exists, and §2 only shows that the naive extensions fail.

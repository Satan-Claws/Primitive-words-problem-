# l6.md — the two-prime uniformity test L₆ = NonPow₂ ∩ NonPow₃ ∩ Σ^{6ℕ}

Scripts:
- `scripts/l6_census.py`: Lemma F check, a census by certificate type, and ambiguity maxima of the partial grammar C_k.
- `scripts/l6_thmS_check.py`: checks the explicit families of Theorem S and Lemma U.

Note that L₆ is exactly the language T of `uniform.md` §4. For n ∈ {6, 12, 18, 24, 36} (n = 2^a·3^b) we have
L₆ ∩ Σⁿ = Q ∩ Σⁿ. For n = 30 they differ.

## 0. Verdict

- **Whether L₆ is CF stays OPEN.** I found no grammar and no non-CF proof.
- **The obstruction is isolated as three [written proof] barriers.** Every natural uniform certificate fails:
  1. **Theorem R.** No CFG can expose a rectangle {i, i+n/3, i+n/2, i+5n/6}, under any pairing or nesting, or any
     other configuration of ≥ 3 points at fixed fractions of n. Such a marked CFL is *finite*. The same holds at the
     block level, so the rectangle covers of `construct.md` cannot be made uniform in N.
  2. **Theorem S.** One chord for one prime plus a *regular* certificate for the other prime cannot cover L₆.
  3. **Lemma F.** Certificates confined to one fibre (positions ≡ r mod n/6) cannot cover L₆. This class includes
     every rectangle and every shared-endpoint triangle. For m ≥ 2 there are "mixed" words that need a 2-witness and
     a 3-witness in different fibres.
- **What survives.** A real certificate must be non-exposed, non-regular and cross-fibre. Run-length comparisons in
  bounded shapes are of this kind, which is why every fixed-shape family, including that of Theorem S, is still CF.
  The remaining question is NONUNIF in letter form.
- **A partial grammar.** C_k is a polynomially ambiguous CFG with L(C_k) ⊆ L₆ and amb ≤ 7n/6 + 1. It covers all of
  L₆ except the "locally 6th-power" words U_k. U_k is non-empty for every k (Lemma U).

## 1. Fibres (Lemma F) [written proof + exhaustive check, n ≤ 18]

**Setup.** Let n = 6m, A = 3m and B = 2m. Fibre r is the vector f_r = (w[r + cm])_{c∈ℤ₆}. A shift by A (resp. B)
maps each fibre to itself as the shift by 3 (resp. 2) on ℤ₆. So:
- w is a square ⇔ every f_r ∈ V₂(6) = {3-periodic};
- w is a cube ⇔ every f_r ∈ V₃(6) = {2-periodic}.

**Single-fibre and mixed words.** A word w ∈ L₆ falls into one of two classes:
- **single-fibre:** some f_r ∉ V₂(6) ∪ V₃(6). Then the six letters of that fibre alone certify w.
- **mixed:** every f_r lies in V₂(6) ∪ V₃(6). Then w ∈ L₆ needs a fibre in V₃∖V₂ *and a different* fibre in V₂∖V₃.

**Consequence.** Any certificate whose points all lie in one fibre misses every mixed word. Such certificates include:
- the rectangle {i, i+B, i+A, i+A+B};
- the triangle w[i] ≠ w[i+A], w[i] ≠ w[i±B];
- the form F_i = w_i − w_{i+A} − w_{i+B} + w_{i+A+B} ≠ 0.

This is the letter-level form of the CRT failure of `construct.md` §2 (gcd(A, B) = m > 1). It is vacuous only at m = 1.

## 2. The partial grammar C_k (polynomially ambiguous, sound) [written proof + computational]

**Definition.** R_{p,k} = {w : some factor f with |f| ≤ k has a cyclic occurrence count ≢ 0 mod p}. It is regular,
and a p-th power never lies in R_{p,k}. Then

  C_k = (G₂ × R_{3,k}) ∪ (G₃ × R_{2,k}) ∪ (R_{2,k} ∩ R_{3,k}), all restricted to Σ^{6ℕ}.

Here G_p is the grammar of `uniform.md`, and × is the product with a DFA, which preserves tree counts.

**Properties.**
- L(C_k) = L₆ ∩ (R_{2,k} ∪ R_{3,k}).
- amb(w) = A₂(w)·[R_{3,k}] + A₃(w)·[R_{2,k}] + [R_{2,k} ∩ R_{3,k}] ≤ n/2 + 2n/3 + 1.
- The ambiguity is linear. Its observed maxima are in the table.
- Theorem W's family a^{n−2}bb lies in R_{3,1} (#b = 2). So C₁ covers it with a single 2-chord. Theorem W limits
  only grammars that expose both witnesses on *every* word; it does not limit L₆ grammars.

**Lemma U [written proof, checked k ≤ 7].** Let u_k = a⁶(b^k a^k)⁶. Then u_k ∈ L₆, because the a-run vector is
(k+6, k, k, k, k, k) up to rotation, which is neither 3- nor 2-periodic. All of its cyclic ≤ k-factor counts are
≡ 0 mod 6: it is a concatenation of 6 + 6 closed walks at the de Bruijn node a^{k−1}, and counts add. So u_k ∈ U_k,
and no C_k equals L₆.

## 3. Theorem S: one chord plus regular is insufficient [written proof; family checked]

**Theorem S.** L₆ is not a finite union of languages of the forms NonSq ∩ R, NonCube ∩ R′ and R″, with R, R′, R″
regular and everything restricted to Σ^{6ℕ}.

*Proof.*
1. Suppose L₆ is such a union. Let N bound the DFA sizes, take L ≥ N, and set D = 6·N!.
2. Let w = a^{L+D} b (a^L b)⁵. Then w ∈ L₆.
3. Every a-run of w contains a loop of every DFA, and D is a multiple of each loop length. So pumping adds exactly D
   letters a to any chosen run, independently in disjoint runs.
4. **If w lies in a piece NonSq ∩ R.** Pump runs 2 and 4. The result is (a^{L+D} b a^L b)³, which lies in R. It is a
   cube and not a square, so it lies in NonSq ∩ R but not in L₆.
5. **If w lies in a piece NonCube ∩ R′.** Pump run 3. The result is (a^{L+D} b a^L b a^L b)², a square and not a cube.
6. **If w lies in a piece R″.** Either pumping gives a contradiction.
7. Every case contradicts soundness. ∎

**What it shows.** For L₆ the second prime cannot be certified by finite-state means once a chord certifies the
first. Theorem S does not show that L₆ is non-CF: L₆ ∩ (a*b)⁶ is CF via run-length comparisons (`construct.md`, n = 6).

## 4. Theorem R: no exposed configuration of ≥ 3 fractional points [written proof]

**Theorem R.** Let G′ be a CFG over Σ × {0,1}. Suppose every word of L(G′) has exactly k marks at positions
p₁ < … < p_k whose gaps g₀ = p₁, g_t = p_{t+1} − p_t − 1, g_k = n − 1 − p_k satisfy |g_t − γ_t·n| ≤ C, for fixed
γ_t ≥ 0 and a fixed C. If at least 3 of the γ_t are positive, then L(G′) is finite.

*Proof.*
1. Apply the homomorphism that sends unmarked letters to c and marked letters to μ. Intersect with
   c*μc*⋯μc*. The result is a bounded CFL.
2. By Ginsburg–Spanier, its exponent set E ⊆ ℕ^{k+1} is a finite union of *stratified* linear sets, so every period
   has support ≤ 2.
3. Take a period P of a linear set c₀ + ℕP ⊆ E. Then g(c₀ + jP) − γ·n(c₀ + jP) = O(1) for all j. This forces
   P_t = γ_t·|P|, so either |P| = 0 or the support of P is ≥ 3.
4. Hence all periods are 0 and E is finite. ∎

**Corollaries.**
- **(i) Rectangles.** The rectangle {i, i+B, i+A, i+A+B} has cyclic gaps (2m, m, 2m, m) − O(1). One gap is split
  by the word boundary, so at least 3 gaps grow. It cannot be exposed, under any pairing: the suggested nested
  pairing (i, i+5m) ⊃ (i+2m, i+3m), the crossing pairing, or the G_p ratio trick, which has only one cut and
  handles exactly one chord.
- **(ii) Other configurations.** The same holds for shared-endpoint triangles, for two chords (this recovers
  Theorem W), and for any single fibre certificate.
- **(iii) Block level.** Apply the homomorphism a ↦ ε, b ↦ c, mark ↦ μ. The argument then rules out exposing blocks
  i, i+N/p, i+N/q, i+N/p+N/q uniformly in the block count N. So the n = pq rectangle covers of `construct.md` and
  the per-N covers of `construct36.md` are inherently per-slice. Any uniform version must hide the rectangle.

## 5. Exact obstruction

A CFG for L₆, if one exists, must certify "non-cube" on non-squares (and vice versa) with a mechanism that is:
- (a) **not finite-state**, by Theorem S;
- (b) **not an exposed configuration** of ≥ 3 fractional points, letter- or block-level, by Theorem R. This rules out
  rectangles and a second chord;
- (c) **not confined to one fibre**, by Lemma F (mixed words exist for every m ≥ 2: 24 at n = 12, 432 at n = 18).

Run-length comparisons (chords between *run lengths*, positions not at fixed fractions) meet (a)–(c). They suffice
for every bounded shape, so a non-CF proof needs words with unboundedly many b-runs and mixed fibres. That is
exactly the NONUNIF frontier. This is [assessment]. **Status: L₆ CF? OPEN; L₆ poly-ambiguous? OPEN.**

**Transfer caveat.** A lower bound for L₆ would not transfer directly to Q, because L₆ ⊋ Q ∩ Σ^{6ℕ}. Restricting to
lengths 2^a3^b is not a CF-preserving operation. Conversely, a uniform upper-bound technique for L₆ would be strong
evidence for the affirmative.

## 6. Three primes (L₃₀ = NonPow₂ ∩ NonPow₃ ∩ NonPow₅ ∩ Σ^{30ℕ})

- **Theorem S extends [written proof sketch].** Take w with a-run vector L·𝟙 + D·e₀ over 30 b's. For each p,
  pumping D into the rest of 0's orbit under the shift by 30/p gives a p-th power that is not a q-th power for
  q ≠ p.
- **Theorem R extends verbatim.**
- **Fibres get worse.** At n = 30 the per-slice obstruction already appears (`construct.md` Lemma O: no ≤ 3-chord
  atom certifies all three primes). Mixed words need three witnesses in three fibres.
- **The C_k analogue still works partially.** One G_p chord plus two regular R_{q,k}, R_{r,k} gives amb = O(n). Its
  uncovered set ⊇ the 30-analogue of U_k.
- **No uniform affirmative mechanism is known for two primes, so none is known for three.**

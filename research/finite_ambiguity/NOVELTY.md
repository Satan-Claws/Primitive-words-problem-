# Novelty check (web search only, Nov 2026)

Network policy blocked drops.dagstuhl.de, arxiv.org, ci.labri.fr, semflajolet.math.cnrs.fr, so the
full texts of Koechlin (FSTTCS 2022), his 2023/2025 slides and Bostan–Carayol–Koechlin–Nicaud
(arXiv 2512.09823) could not be read. Verdicts rest on search snippets.

* **Main theorem (Q not finitely ambiguous): NOT FOUND.** No search result states that Q is
  inherently infinitely ambiguous / not finitely ambiguous. Every source found gives only Petersen's
  "not unambiguous" (1994) and calls context-freeness of Q open.
* **Lemma 3 (Structure lemma): ESSENTIALLY KNOWN.** Ginsburg–Ullian (JACM 1966, doi 10.1145/321356.321366),
  as quoted by Koechlin 2022: a bounded CFL has a k-ambiguous grammar iff its semilinear set is a
  finite union of stratified linear sets with independent periods such that every intersection of
  more than k of them is empty. Our Lemma 3 (multiplicity = sum of indicators of laminar simple
  linear sets) is the necessity half of this, proved independently. The proof may cite G–U instead.
* **Slice R_m with two marker blocks, Lemma X, and the half-space covering argument: NOT FOUND.**
  These are the parts that would be new.
* **To do before any claim of novelty:** read Koechlin 2022 (Props 14, 17 and the interlacing criterion),
  his 2025 Flajolet-seminar slides, Ginsburg–Ullian 1966, and the Dömösi–Ito book (2014) in full.
